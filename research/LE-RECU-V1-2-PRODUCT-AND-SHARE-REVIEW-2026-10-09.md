# LE REÇU — Duplex V1.1 → V1.2 product/visual review

Date: 2026-10-09  
Status: **REALITY-GATED CREATIVE REVIEW — NOT FINAL CONCEPT LOCK**  
Base: one observed cross-browser live completion, production not released.

## Direct product judgment

The 4:5 artifact has a coherent, recognizable editorial language: warm perforated receipt, ink red refusal, a dramatic pair of choices, and a tight verbal contrast. Its social rule is unusually legible: same desire / five costs / two independent refusals. The one verified two-browser Neon commitment moves it past static AI art.

**What is not demonstrated:** actual uncoached comprehension, repeat play, retention, forwarding from a finished artifact, clickthrough from a social feed, user-generated organic challenges, distinct persons, or any viral coefficient. A share-sheet opening is not distribution.

## Recommended product experiment after runtime quality

Build a clean, public **new duel** invitation path *only after* we have a release-safe domain and backend guardrails. Never use a public completed-duel URL exposing private choices. Compare three independent experiences by equal-quality presentations:

- **The Disagreement:** two independently chosen refusals, receipt reveals the exact collision. Preserve as control with better art.
- **The Chain:** after responding, the recipient can originate their own new challenge, with opt-in chain continuity and no personal free text. Ask whether this creates actual organic next invitations, not just clicks.
- **The Conversation:** after the reveal, a tiny non-coercive prompt invites the pair to explain *why* that cost mattered privately; avoid mandatory confession or publishing sensitive text.

Qualify using uncoached users, actual invite→open→reply→next-invite conversion, errors, return value, and meaningful discussion. Do not optimize screenshots and self-traffic until users actually understand the two personal prices.
